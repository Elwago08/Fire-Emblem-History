A Brief History of the Fire Emblem Series (Continued)

Revival

With the series on the brink of demise, Intelligent systems went to work to make something that would hit that 250,000 units number. The game they made was Fire Emblem: Awakening,which ended up selling a total of 2.3 million units worldwide, destroying the required goal! This game managed to restore faith in Nintendo to continue investing in the series, with new titles consistently selling well over a million copies worldwide. And with new games coming out, it likely won't be leaving anytime soon.
